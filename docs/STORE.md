**Promotional text:**
Free the cars, fill the seats, send everyone on their way. Collect 22 pixel cars as you go, from a tiny hatchback to a golden supercar.

**Description:**

Parxi is a color puzzle about getting everyone home.

The parking lot is packed with colorful cars, buses and big rigs, and every vehicle can only drive forward. Riders wait in line at the stop. Tap a vehicle with a clear road: it drives out and pulls into the stop, and riders of the same color hop in. Once it's full, off it goes. But the stop has only a few spots, so think about which vehicle to free first.

WHY PARXI?
• Every level is solvable: no impossible puzzles, and hints always work.
• There is always one spare spot in the solution: tight, never unfair.
• Play it smart: keep the stop as empty as possible for 3 stars.
• Cars, buses and big rigs, each with their own number of seats.

NEW CHALLENGES UNLOCK
• Mystery vehicles show their color only when their road is clear.
• Barriers lift after enough vehicles have left.
• Locked vehicles wait for a key color to hit the road.
• VIP riders won't wait long at the front of the line.
• A Hard level every 10 levels, with double rewards.
• Bigger lots and more colors as you climb.

COLLECT PIXEL CARS
• 22 hand-made pixel cars unlock as you beat levels: taxi, police car, fire truck, limo, monster truck, rocket car and a golden supercar.
• The best ones wait further ahead. Put your favorite on display.

A REASON TO COME BACK
• Daily Lot: the same puzzle for everyone. Keep your streak and share your result.
• Daily missions and a reward chest.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Spend coins on themes and car styles: Glossy, Retro, Neon.
• Color-blind mode adds a symbol to every color.
• Adjust the boarding speed.

FAIR ADS PROMISE

Parxi, herkesi evine ulaştırmaya çalıştığın bir renk bulmacası.

Otopark renkli arabalar, otobüsler ve tırlarla dolu, ve her araç yalnızca ileri gidebiliyor. Durakta yolcular sıra bekliyor. NEDEN PARXI?
• Her seviye çözülebilir: imkânsız bulmaca yok, ipucu her zaman çalışır.
